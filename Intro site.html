What should you do when you run into a Pokemon?

Rule number 1!:

Do not be afraid! This site makes sure to prepare you with pokeballs and knowledge.

Rule number 2!:

At the top of the page, you’ll find the “Generate Encounter” button.
Clicking it creates a new wild Pokémon, which appears in the center of the screen.
Once a Pokémon appears, you have two options:

1. "Catch it" using the pokeball at the buttom.

2. Or look for another Pokemon by clicking "Generate Encounter" again

Tips:

On the left, you’ll see a list of strategy books that can help you understand Pokémon types, strengths, and tactics.

In the middle, the encountered Pokémon is displayed along with important stats such as typing.
These help you judge whether the Pokémon is worth catching.

On the right, you can see the current location and weather for the encounter.
Weather affects different Pokémon types differently, and the table below explains how each type reacts to different conditions.

If you want a more immersive experience, you can click the green unmute button to turn on background music
to get battle themes during encounters and calm music afterwards.

At the bottom, you’ll find a collection of all the Pokémon you’ve caught, each shown with its name and image.
This list is also saved locally, so you won’t lose your progress if you take a break.

Ready? Press the pokeball to start your adventure!
